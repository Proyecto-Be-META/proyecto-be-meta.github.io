Dr. **Samuel Nucamendi** is undertaking an international research stay aimed at collaborating on research projects, strengthening academic networks and generating knowledge with impact.

Since the start of his stay, he has been collaborating within the **Be-META** project on solving routing problems and modelling problems in healthcare. This collaboration brings together research interests in optimisation and the representation of complex problems arising in health services.

The stay provides an opportunity to exchange knowledge and approaches, strengthen links between research teams and explore shared lines of work. It also highlights the value of international academic mobility in supporting scientific cooperation.
