Professor Iván Castilla attended the [*International Multidisciplinary Modeling & Simulation Multiconference*](https://www.msc-les.org/i3m2026/) (I3M 2026) in Genoa, where he presented the paper *"An Agent Based Digital Twin Design for Surgical Waiting List Management: From Theoretical Approaches to Real Development”*, developed within the **Be-META** project. The research moves from theoretical design towards the development of a digital twin for surgical waiting list management.

The paper proposes an architecture that combines software agents, an ontology-based communication framework and a human-in-the-loop approach to decision-making.

This contribution builds on the design presented the previous year and stems from Julián Garrido Arana’s master’s thesis. Julián accompanied Professor Iván Castilla to the conference to share this research and take his first steps into the research community.

This edition held particular commemorative significance, as it took place in the home city of the conference’s founder, Professor Agostino Bruzzone, who passed away the previous year. His commitment to fostering collaboration in modelling and simulation was remembered at an event that brought together experts and researchers from various disciplines.
