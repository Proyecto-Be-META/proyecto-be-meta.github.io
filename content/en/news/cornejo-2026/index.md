José Alejandro Cornejo Acosta delivered the talk “Metaheuristics for routing problems and their application to hospital management” during his short research stay collaborating with members of the **Parallel Algorithms and Languages (PAL) Research Group**, within the **Be-META** project.

The talk addressed the use of optimisation techniques to solve transport, mobility and logistics problems, as well as their adaptation to challenges in the hospital sector.

Routing problems, such as the travelling salesman problem (TSP) and the vehicle routing problem (VRP), have been studied for decades owing to their practical relevance and computational complexity. Among the techniques developed to tackle them, **metaheuristics** offer approximate solutions for large instances where exact methods may be impractical.

However, traditional TSP and VRP formulations cannot always capture all the requirements of real-world applications. This motivates the development of adaptations and extensions that incorporate the specific characteristics of each context.

The session connected the foundations of metaheuristic optimisation for classical routing problems with their potential extension to more complex scenarios. In particular, it explored their application to **hospital management**, a field that presents new challenges for modelling and solving optimisation problems.
