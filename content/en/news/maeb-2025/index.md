Professor Eduardo Segredo attended the [XVI Congreso Español de Metaheurísticas, Algoritmos Evolutivos y Bioinspirados](https://www.uik.eus/es/curso/xvi-congreso-espanol-metaheuristicas-algoritmos-evolutivos-bioinspirados) (MAEB 2025), held from 28 to 30 May 2025 in Donostia-San Sebastián, where he presented two contributions developed within the **Be-META** project. The contributions address instance generation for evaluating optimisation algorithms and the integrated planning of hospital resources.

The first, [*"DIGNEA: A tool to generate diverse and discriminatory instances for optimisation domains"*](https://portalciencia.ull.es/documentos/689a30e1fe823d1bf8525694), presents a tool that combines evolutionary algorithms with novelty search techniques to generate diverse instances that distinguish the performance of different solvers. The paper illustrates its application to the knapsack problem and describes its availability in C++, as a Docker image and in a Python version.

The second contribution, [*"Memetic computing for healthcare resource management: an integrated planning for operating theaters, rooms, and continuity of care"*](https://portalciencia.ull.es/documentos/68963b987d435e37fc5fe137), takes an integrated approach to several healthcare planning problems: operating theatre allocation, hospital admission planning and the assignment of nursing staff to patient rooms. The research proposes using a memetic algorithm to obtain high-quality solutions that help improve operational efficiency and healthcare resource management.

The event took place at the Carlos Santamaría building on the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU) campus and provided a forum for exchanging ideas and discussing metaheuristics and bioinspired algorithms. Participation offered an opportunity to disseminate these Be-META research strands and share the proposed approaches with the scientific community.
