Darius Bereznitchi, a student at Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași (Romania), worked with Professors Iván Castilla and Eduardo Segredo for two months through a traineeship funded by the **Erasmus+** programme. As part of this collaboration, he visited the **Escuela Superior de Ingeniería y Tecnología de la Universidad de La Laguna** from 28 September to 11 October 2026.

The work, undertaken within the **Be-META** research project, focused on developing and studying digital twins for managing surgical waiting lists and hospital bed occupancy. These tools enable healthcare processes to be represented and scenarios to be explored to support planning and decision-making in healthcare.

During the collaboration, Bereznitchi implemented data visualisation systems and dashboards, as well as interactive web-based three-dimensional simulations. The work also involved applying data analysis, statistical and machine learning techniques to predict waiting times and demand for healthcare services.

His contribution addressed the identification of bottlenecks in hospital processes and the optimisation of hospital operations through data-driven decision support systems and scenario analysis tools. The traineeship helped advance Be-META’s research and promote knowledge exchange between the two universities.
