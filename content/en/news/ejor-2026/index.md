The *European Journal of Operational Research* has published the paper [*"A matheuristic augmented ε-constraint framework for a bi-objective Multi-Depot Cumulative Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem"*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2026.06.022), a study conducted within the **Be-META** project that proposes an optimisation approach to route planning in complex logistics contexts.

The study addresses a bi-objective variant of the vehicle routing problem involving multiple depots and capacity constraints. In this context, decision-making requires analysing trade-offs between conflicting objectives related to operational efficiency and customer service performance.

To tackle this problem, the study proposes a framework that combines mathematical programming and heuristic search strategies. This approach enables the generation and analysis of efficient solutions, offering a range of alternatives to support decision-making.

The contribution highlights the importance of providing a structured view of trade-offs between objectives. This perspective is particularly relevant to transport and distribution applications in which service quality, waiting times and resource utilisation must be considered simultaneously.

The research was conducted by Eduardo Segredo, Samuel Nucamendi, Gara Miranda and Eduardo Lalla-Ruiz, and was supported by the Be-META project (PID2023-152614OB-I00), funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by “ERDF/EU”.
