Professor Iván Castilla attended the [International Multidisciplinary Modeling & Simulation Multiconference 2025](https://www.msc-les.org/i3m2025/), held in Fez, Morocco, from 17 to 19 September 2025. During the event, he presented a digital twin prototype aimed at improving surgical waiting list management, developed within the **Be-META** project.

Participation in the conference provided an opportunity to showcase progress on the prototype and share the work with the international community of modelling and simulation specialists. The event also offered a forum for exchanging experiences and perspectives on research in this field.

During his stay, Professor Iván Castilla had the opportunity to reconnect with colleagues and experts who share his research interests.

The event was marked by the recent passing of Professor Agostino Bruzzone, its driving force, just days before the conference began. His memory was honoured during an edition that brought the scientific community together to exchange knowledge and collaborate.
