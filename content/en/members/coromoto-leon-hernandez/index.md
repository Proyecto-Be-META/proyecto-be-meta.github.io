Dr. Coromoto León is a Full Professor of Computer Languages and Systems at the University of La Laguna (ULL) in Tenerife, Spain. She earned her Bachelor's degree in Mathematics in 1990 and completed her Ph.D. in Computer Engineering in 1996 at ULL, focusing her doctoral thesis on parallel programming languages and algorithmic model implementations.

Throughout her academic career, Dr. León has established herself as a prominent researcher in high-performance computing, optimization algorithms, and parallel programming. As a founding member and senior researcher of the Parallel Algorithms and Languages (PAL) group, she has led numerous competitive national and international research projects.

Beyond core computer science, Dr. León is deeply committed to educational innovation. She actively promotes computational thinking across primary and secondary education levels as founder of the Cultural Chair of Computational Thinking at ULL. In addition to her technical and pedagogical contributions, she is an advocate for gender diversity, inclusion in STEM fields, and support for high-ability students. Dr. León has authored over 100 scientific publications and frequently collaborates on initiatives to foster digital literacy and bridge the gender gap in technology.
