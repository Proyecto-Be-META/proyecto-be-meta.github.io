Rafael Herrero Álvarez holds a PhD in Computer Engineering from the University of La Laguna, specialising in the application of emerging technologies to education. His research focuses on computational thinking, adaptive learning systems, artificial intelligence and the analysis of emotions in teaching and learning processes.

He has conducted his research at the University of La Laguna, contributing to educational innovation and science outreach projects. He has authored several publications in international scientific journals and contributed to conferences specialising in computing, technology and teaching innovation.

His teaching experience includes programming, artificial intelligence and digital skills, as well as the design of learning resources and active learning methodologies. His academic background includes postgraduate studies in education, information technology and teacher training.

His professional work combines research, teaching and science outreach, with a particular interest in making technology accessible to society and promoting innovative, inclusive education that responds to new digital challenges.
