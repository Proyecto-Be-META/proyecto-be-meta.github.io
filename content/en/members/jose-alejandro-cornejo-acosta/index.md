José Alejandro obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the Instituto Nacional de Astrofísica, Óptica y Electrónica (INAOE), Mexico, where he conducted research on computational optimisation using heuristic techniques and exact methods. He has worked as a software engineer for several companies and as a lecturer, teaching courses in software development and mathematics.

His current research focuses on the design and development of optimisation techniques for problems involving high-dimensional graphs. He has published book chapters and articles in internationally recognised journals, with notable contributions to graph optimisation problems. He has also participated in national and international conferences and academic events, delivering presentations and specialised workshops on optimisation, and has served as a reviewer for international journals in the field.

José Alejandro is passionate about algorithmic programming and is an advocate of free and open-source software. He has contributed to major open-source projects related to graph algorithms and optimisation. He is currently a SECIHTI postdoctoral researcher based at the Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas (CIMAT), where he collaborates with the Numerical Methods, Parallel Computing and Optimisation research group in the Department of Computer Science.
