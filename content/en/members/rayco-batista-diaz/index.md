Rayco Batista Diaz holds a degree in Computer Engineering and is a software architect and PhD student in Artificial Intelligence. He is currently co-founder and CEO of Pathmonk, a technology company based in Germany, where he develops AI-powered products to optimise the user experience on the web.

His academic background includes a degree in Computer Engineering, with a final-year project on genetic programming; a master’s degree in Computer Science specialising in Network Security and Distributed Web Applications; and a specialisation in Data Science focusing on applied statistics, modelling and machine learning. He is currently pursuing a PhD in Artificial Intelligence, researching behavioural analysis in event streams and transformer-based models, with a focus on practical applications in product development and engineering. His profile is complemented by Professional Scrum Master certification and participation in Y Combinator’s Startup School.

With more than fifteen years of experience, his career spans seed-stage start-ups and major Fortune 500 companies, particularly in the fintech and security sectors. He has led multinational engineering organisations comprising multiple teams. His research interests connect his early enthusiasm for evolutionary computation with his current work, focusing on how transformer architectures can model and help explain user behaviour through sequences of events, bridging academic research and its application in real-world products.
