His main research interests include mathematical modelling and the development of exact, approximate and hybrid algorithms to solve optimisation problems in logistics, transport and planning.

He has published a large number of articles in prestigious scientific journals and has received more than 3,500 citations (Google Scholar), nearly 3,000 of them since 2021, demonstrating the continued relevance and high impact of his work.
